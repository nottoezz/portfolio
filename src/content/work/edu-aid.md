## The brief

Edu-Aid’s handwriting fonts (Grade R–2 print, dotted tracing, arrow-guided and cursive) have been used by South African teachers and occupational therapists for two decades. They needed a modern way to sell them directly, online, without the buying process getting in the way.

## What I did

I designed and built the storefront end to end: the positioning, the page structure, the pricing and licence presentation, and the checkout-to-delivery flow.

## The hard parts

- Explaining a subtle problem (generic fonts teach the wrong letter shapes) to busy teachers in one glance.
- Presenting individual and school licences without making the pricing confusing.
- Being found: the site had to rank for high-intent searches like “Grade 1 handwriting font South Africa”.

## Approach

A bold, benefit-led headline with one clear price and a free trial for people who aren’t sure yet. Trust signals sit up front (CAPS-aligned, DOE-approved, 20+ years in classrooms), backed by solid technical SEO and social previews.
