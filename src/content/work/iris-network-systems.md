## The brief

Iris builds serious network-monitoring software for service providers and enterprises: performance monitoring, NetFlow analytics and live maps. Their old site undersold that. It was dated, hard to scan, and made technical services feel harder than they are.

## What I did

**UX & structure.** I audited the old site, simplified the information architecture, and reworked the copy hierarchy around strong hero messaging and scannable sections.

**Front-end engineering.** I built a React single-page app with Tailwind, with responsive navigation and config-driven components so new pages can be assembled without starting from scratch.

## The hard parts

- Translating a legacy layout into something modern but still familiar to existing customers.
- Optimising for performance and responsiveness without stripping out the product imagery.
- Making technical network services feel approachable to buyers who aren’t technical.

## Approach

A component-based, mobile-first layout. Benefit-led content, clear calls to action, lean assets and better image handling.
