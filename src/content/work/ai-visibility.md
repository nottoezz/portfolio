## The brief

Most SEO tools are built for SEO professionals. The founders and marketers I worked with just wanted to know what was wrong with their site, why it mattered, and what to fix first.

## What I did

**Product & UX.** I defined the core flows, designed a minimal, high-contrast UI, and wrote all the UX copy for non-technical founders.

**Engineering.** I built the dashboard, the scanning engine, background jobs, and email and lead automation workflows.

## What it did

It crawled a website and gathered performance, SEO, accessibility and best-practice metrics in one place. It ran real Lighthouse audits on real URLs instead of lab-perfect demo pages, then highlighted the high-impact issues and explained why they mattered.
